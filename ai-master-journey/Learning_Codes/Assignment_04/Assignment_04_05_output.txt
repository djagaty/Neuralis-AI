Part 1: adding a length-3 vector to every row of a 3x3 array
array =
[[ 2 15 13]
 [ 9  9 17]
 [ 2 14  4]]
vector = [ 2 11 19]
result =
[[ 4 26 32]
 [11 20 36]
 [ 4 25 23]]

Part 2: subtracting a length-4 vector from every column of a 4x4 array
array =
[[14 15 14 15]
 [10  3 16  9]
 [10  8  4 18]
 [15 13  8 16]]
vector = [11  9  9  5]
result =
[[ 3  4  3  4]
 [ 1 -6  7  0]
 [ 1 -1 -5  9]
 [10  8  3 11]]
